⚖️ WGe 1

¹ Evil counteth not when one maketh the wrongdoer
² do her crimes, but when suffereth
³ another person, evil counteth.

⚖️ WGe 2

¹ The moral desire is a willingness to take goodness for thyself, and
² and unwillingness to receive evil.

⚖️ WGe 3

¹ Although thou wantest to take and receive all goodness,
² take it not from others,
³ for others also desire goodness, just like
⁴ thou.
